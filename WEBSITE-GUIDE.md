## Prepare your website

Check the application's required PHP version, extensions and database compatibility. List the domains, databases and mailboxes you need before choosing a plan. Include email storage and uploads when estimating disk use.

For an existing site, copy the site files and database, then check URLs, application settings and email separately. Test the new copy before changing DNS. Fasting Host does not provide a migration service, so arrange the move yourself or with your developer.

## Register a domain or keep your current registrar

You can register an available domain, request an eligible transfer or keep your existing registrar and connect its DNS to your hosting. A hosting move does not require a domain transfer.

Review the separate registration, transfer and renewal prices. For a transfer, confirm the extension's requirements, keep access to the registrant email and obtain an authorization code where applicable. Copy essential DNS records before changing nameservers so website and email services continue to resolve correctly.

Use the [live domain service page](https://fasting.host/domains) for prices and extension conditions. Hosting's annual two-free-month offer does not apply to domain terms.

## Free AutoSSL or optional paid SSL?

| Option | When to consider it |
| --- | --- |
| Included free AutoSSL | Ordinary HTTPS for eligible websites on Fasting Host cPanel hosting, with working DNS and successful domain validation. |
| Optional paid certificate | A project that requires a specific RapidSSL, GeoTrust or DigiCert product, organization validation or suitable wildcard coverage. |

Paid SSL is optional. Select a certificate for its actual coverage and validation requirements rather than assuming that a price alone makes HTTPS stronger. OV/EV products require issuer checks; every public certificate requires domain-control validation.

Confirm all names the certificate must cover. A purchased service term can include revalidation and reissuance; it does not mean one certificate remains valid for the whole term. VPS customers configure their own HTTPS and renewal process unless assistance is separately agreed.

[Compare current SSL products](https://fasting.host/ssl) and read the [certificate policy](https://fasting.host/legal#ssl) before ordering.

## Before you launch

Test your homepage, navigation, forms, sign-in, checkout if applicable, mobile layout, HTTPS, images and transactional email. Remove development-only pages and sample content. Keep your application, plugins and themes maintained after launch.
