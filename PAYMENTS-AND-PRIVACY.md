# Payments, privacy and service policies

[Back to README](README.md) · [Official payment guide](https://fasting.host/payments) · [Policy centre](https://fasting.host/legal)

## Card and cryptocurrency payments

Fasting Host accepts cryptocurrency through NOWPayments, including BTC and XMR, and eligible card payments. Use the methods and instructions available for your particular checkout or invoice.

For crypto, verify the coin, network, destination, amount, expiry and any memo/tag. Allow for fees and confirmations. An incomplete or held payment can delay activation. Never reuse an old invoice's payment instructions.

## Fasting Host's no-KYC policy

Fasting Host does not request government-issued ID, identity selfies or biometric checks. Account, contact and billing information are still needed.

Independent payment providers may apply transaction checks, including identity or source-of-funds verification for flagged payments. Domain registrars and certificate issuers also have their own validation requirements. Fasting Host's policy cannot waive those separate requirements.

## Billing, refunds and responsible use

Annual VPS/cPanel billing provides twelve months for ten monthly payments. The published policy includes a seven-day refund guarantee for newly purchased services; read its conditions and request process before ordering. Domain and paid SSL terms are separate from the annual hosting offer.

Privacy-focused offshore hosting remains subject to lawful-use, abuse-handling and service policies. Read the current policies for full terms.
