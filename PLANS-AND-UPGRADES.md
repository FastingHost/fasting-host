## CPU and memory additions

Choose **0–15 additional CPU units** and **0–15 additional whole GB of RAM** above your base plan. Zero keeps the base resources. These are additions, not the total CPU/RAM of the resulting service.

| Addition | Privacy Cloud VPS / month | Performance Cloud VPS / month | cPanel / month |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| +1 vCPU / CPU core-equivalent limit | $5 | $3 | $4 |
| +1 GB RAM / account RAM limit | $3 | $2 | $3 |

For example, adding 3 vCPU and 4 GB RAM to a Performance VPS costs **$17/month extra**: `3 × $3 + 4 × $2`. Annual recurring additions cost ten times the monthly addition.
