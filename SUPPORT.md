# Contact and support

[Back to README](README.md)

## Sales and account support

- **Online:** [Fasting Host contact and ticket options](https://fasting.host/contact)
- **Email:** [support@fasting.host](mailto:support@fasting.host)
- **Existing services and invoices:** [Fasting Host client area](https://fasting.host/clientarea/)
- **Common questions:** [Official FAQ](https://fasting.host/faq)

Support is available 24/7. This does not promise a fixed response or resolution time. No migration service is offered.

## Help us understand your request

Include the service reference or affected public URL, a short description, the time with timezone, what you expected and the error you received. For a custom VPS quote, include the workload, OS, CPU, RAM, disk needs and audience location.

Remove passwords, private SSH keys, API keys, full card details and wallet recovery phrases from messages and screenshots. Use the official support channel for account-specific information.

## Documentation improvements

GitHub issues and pull requests are for corrections and useful additions to these public guides. They are public and are not a private account-support channel. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before submitting.

## Security, privacy and abuse reports

Use support@fasting.host with a clear subject such as **Security report**, **Privacy request** or **Abuse report**. Describe the affected service and relevant evidence without posting secrets or harmful attachments. Follow the [reporting and privacy policies](https://fasting.host/legal).

For company details, service scope, billing, refunds or complaints, read the [Fasting Host policy centre](https://fasting.host/legal).
