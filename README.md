## Cloud VPS: build with control

- VMware virtualization with root or Administrator access, appropriate to your selected OS.
- NVME M.2/SSD storage, with capacities shown for each plan.
- One public IPv4 address with each standard VPS; extra addresses can be requested.
- Linux, FreeBSD and Windows choices according to the service family and plan.
- Password-based access, with an optional SSH public key.
- DDoS protection and international connectivity from Netherlands infrastructure.
- Selectable resource additions and custom configurations, including enquiries for 128 GB+ RAM.

Standard VPS plans are unmanaged: you maintain the guest OS, applications and your recovery arrangements. OS eligibility, availability and network performance depend on the chosen service. Unlimited* traffic follows the [Fair Use Policy](https://fasting.host/legal#fair-use).

## cPanel hosting: useful tools included

Use cPanel, LiteSpeed and LSCache to run compatible websites, with Imunify360, CloudLinux and CageFS. The plans include WordPress Toolkit, a PHP version selector, database tools, email and webmail, FTP/File Manager, and unlimited subdomains within account resources.

Eligible hosted domains include **free automatic SSL through AutoSSL**. You do not need to buy a paid certificate for ordinary HTTPS on eligible cPanel hosting. JetBackup restore access depends on available restore points; keep an independent copy of important data.

## Common questions

**Where are the VPS servers?** In Amsterdam and Eygelshoven, Netherlands. Customers can use the services for projects serving a worldwide audience.

**Do I need a VPS for WordPress?** A compatible WordPress website can use cPanel hosting. A VPS suits projects that need their own server environment and OS control.

**Can I use my existing domain?** Yes. A domain transfer is optional; you can keep your registrar and connect the domain through DNS.

**Can I request a larger server?** Yes. The published plans are starting configurations. Contact sales about larger requirements and availability.

**Are free migrations included?** No migration service is offered. Plan the transfer of your site, databases and email before changing DNS.

**Where do I manage my service?** Use the [Fasting Host client area](https://fasting.host/clientarea/). For questions, use the [online contact form](https://fasting.host/contact).
